Privacy Policy

LifeDesk · Effective 13 November 2026

Summary

LifeDesk does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

Who we are

LifeDesk is a personal organiser for expenses, income, bills, documents, tasks, subscriptions and passwords, published by LifeDesk. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the developer, and "you" refers to the person using the app.

Information you enter

You may enter or attach the following, all of which stays on your device:

  • Money records: transactions, amounts, categories, notes, accounts and opening balances.
  • Documents: photos and PDF files you choose to attach, such as bills, receipts and warranties.
  • Tasks: titles, notes, due dates and priorities.
  • Subscriptions: service names, prices and renewal dates.
  • Password vault: website or app names, usernames and passwords.
  • Preferences: your display name, currency, theme and similar settings.

We never receive a copy of any of this information.

Where your data is stored

Your records are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Passwords in the vault are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your vault PIN (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). Your PIN is never stored in readable form. The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your records are not copied to any cloud service by the operating system.

Information we collect

None. The app does not contain analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not request the internet permission. We cannot see how you use the app, and we have no server where your information could be stored.

Backups you create

You can export an encrypted backup file at any time. The file is protected with a passphrase you choose and is saved wherever you pick using Android's file picker, for example your Downloads folder or a cloud storage app you already use. Once you save or share a backup file, it is handled by the storage location or app you chose, under that provider's own terms. We cannot recover a backup if the passphrase is lost.

Exports and sharing

When you export a PDF statement or CSV file, or share a document, the app creates the file on your device and opens Android's share screen. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose a destination.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you before a subscription renews or a saved document expires. You can turn this off in Android settings at any time.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for light feedback when you tap certain controls. You can turn this off in Settings.

Files and photos are only accessed when you pick them using Android's system picker. The clipboard is only read when you tap Paste inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and control

Because your data never leaves your device, you are fully in control of it. You can edit or delete any record in the app, erase everything from Settings > Erase all data, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app. Data that has been erased cannot be recovered unless you have your own backup file.

Security

We use the security features provided by Android, along with an optional app PIN lock and on-device encryption for the password vault. No method of storage is completely secure, so please protect your phone with a screen lock and keep your PINs and backup passphrase private.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in an app update and published on the store listing with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@lifedesk.app.
